The pyramids of Giza were royal tombs built during Egypt’s Old Kingdom. Their immense scale reflects generations of planning, skilled labor, and a belief in the pharaoh’s journey beyond this life.

Egyptian writing ranged from everyday hieratic script to the formal hieroglyphs carved on temples and tombs. Scribes recorded royal histories, religious rituals, trade, and the rhythms of daily life.

Many ancient Egyptians believed that life continued after death. Elaborate burials, offerings, and funerary texts helped prepare the deceased for a passage through the underworld and into the next world.
